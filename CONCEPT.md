# BattleReport — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

A personal combat-history and analysis addon for WoW 1.12, displaying a live and post-fight report and averages across up to 100 fights.

## Source-supported design principles

The focus is one character's damage, healing, defenses, resource flow and identifiable effects—not a group DPS leaderboard; separately named pets and totems are not automatically attributed.

Reconstructed from README, named source files and tests, not original chat records.
